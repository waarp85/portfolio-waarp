About the Service

I turn title artwork into 3D, then light and animate it for music videos, tours and series: titles that catch the light, move with the music and sit inside the shot instead of on top of it.

I start from the title artwork, usually delivered as vectors by the designer, and take it all the way to the final frames: 3D build, look development, animation and compositing into live-action footage, working directly with artists, labels, directors and production companies.

Freelance 3D generalist based in Italy, working remotely with brands, labels, creative studios and agencies worldwide, from 3D build and look development to lighting, rendering and animation.
