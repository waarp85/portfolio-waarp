What's inside

One panel, live in the Live Viewer

Colour

Contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, vibrance, saturation, hue and HSL per colour.

Colour wheels

Shadows, midtones and highlights, the classic lift, gamma and gain.

Curves

An RGB curve plus separate red, green and blue curves.

Looks

Load any .cube LUT, or just drop it on the panel. Save your grades as looks and reuse them.

Object masks

Grade a single object on its own: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, temperature, tint, hue, saturation.

Finish

Clarity, sharpen, noise and vignette for the last touch.

Before / After

One click to compare the graded image with the original.

Native Octane

Finisher drives Octane's own compositor. Exposure, white balance, bloom and glare stay in the camera tag, as usual.

Install in 3 steps

  1. 1. Close Cinema 4D and unzip the download.
  2. 2. Copy the Finisher for OctaneRender folder into the C4D 2027 plugins folder. Not sure where it is? In Cinema 4D: Preferences > Open Preferences Folder.
  3. 3. Restart Cinema 4D and open Extensions > Finisher for OctaneRender.

The full guide is in the README inside the zip.

Good to know

  • Your camera needs an Octane Camera tag. Click Create stack and pick Finisher's Out tab in the Live Viewer.
  • Real-time mode: turn Denoised off in the panel.
  • Transparent Out: turn Keep environment off in Octane's kernel settings.
  • Sending a scene to a render farm or a colleague: turn Noise off, or install Finisher there too.
  • With the camera tag's imager off, the Out follows Octane Settings > Imager, and the vignette is set there.
  • With Post processing on in the camera tag, bloom and glare show in the Out tab, not in Main.
  • Other versions of C4D and Octane, and macOS, may work but haven't been tested yet.
